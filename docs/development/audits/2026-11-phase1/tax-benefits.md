## Prioritized findings

### TB-1 · High · Proven defect by static arithmetic · NIIT MAGI uses the uncapped net capital loss

**Requirement and source.** PFA-TAX-001/010 require the applicable NIIT liability. IRC §1411(a), (b), (d) compares net investment income with the excess of modified AGI, with a distinct AGI computation. IRC §1211(b) limits an individual's ordinary-income capital-loss deduction. The pinned federal catalog cites [IRS NIIT guidance](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/net-investment-income-tax) and [IRS Topic 409](https://www.irs.gov/taxtopics/tc409) at `src/rules/tax/lawCatalog.ts:103–130,140–167`.

**Path and impact.** `src/simulation/tax/participant.ts:382–387` constructs MAGI from wages, interest, dividends and signed gains by taking the full realized loss. It passes this to `applyTaxCoreRule`. `src/rules/tax/calculator.ts:95–116` correctly caps the loss for the ordinary base, while `:149–152` uses the supplied MAGI unchanged for NIIT. With an ordinary realized loss and positive investment income, this can understate payable tax and overstate after-tax net worth or refunds.

**Independent oracle.** Full-year 2026 single filer, with base-deduction-only eligibility and no other adjustments, $190,000 wages, $40,000 taxable interest and $20,000 long-term realized investment loss, with admitted source facts. AGI/MAGI is $227,000 ($230,000 less the $3,000 deductible loss), leaving $27,000 above the $200,000 threshold. With a $3,000 allowable net loss on Form 8960 and no other adjustments, NII is $37,000 and NIIT is $1,026.00. As an isolated MAGI check, even holding NII fixed at the participant's $20,000, the correct MAGI produces $760.00. The participant supplies $210,000 MAGI and $20,000 NII, producing $380.00. Assert the NIIT component, federal liability journal and final balance under zero withholding. Use a recognized disposition tax fact for the negative gain, or inject it through the same participant observation interface in a narrow reproduction. [IRS Form 8960 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i8960).

**False-positive checks.** `t1aTaxCore.test.ts:80` supplies already-resolved MAGI and NII, and `t1aTaxIntegration.test.ts:555–562` uses positive interest without a loss. Both can pass with this defect. The separately merged T1B carryover evaluator (#154) does not supply the participant's MAGI. #198 and #236 address paycheck credit and cash presentation. This is a baseline annual calculation issue for the tax authority, with #149 owning the shared revalidation. No runtime case was executed.

### TB-2 · Medium · Proven defect by static arithmetic · Denver monthly threshold partitions one employee's wages by unrelated facts

**Requirement and source.** PFA-TAX-001/002/010 and the catalog's Denver rule specify one monthly $5.75 employee OPT charge when eligible Denver compensation reaches $500, once across employers. Scope and effective start are recorded at `src/rules/tax/lawCatalog.ts:69,77–82`. [Denver Treasury Tax Guide Topic 61](https://www.denvergov.org/files/assets/public/v/2/finance/documents/treasury/tax-guides/taxguidetopic61_occupationalprivilegetaxes_1.pdf).

**Path and impact.** `src/simulation/tax/participant.ts:198–207,283–285,309–313` groups periodic liability by jurisdiction, rule, employee, month **and the entire facts object**, including residence, service PSD/municipality and unrelated eligibility. The calculator applies the full threshold and flat charge to each group (`src/rules/tax/calculator.ts:154–158`), then the participant sums the amounts (`participant.ts:388–392`). A single eligible employee can miss the threshold or incur it twice despite the same total legal Denver wages.

**Independent oracle.** One employee with two April 2026 $250.00 Denver wage streams from distinct employers, explicit affirmative Denver eligibility and otherwise valid common facts. Add an unrelated, nonconflicting eligibility fact to just one Income, leaving the same Denver rule and work city. Expected $5.75 on $500.00 total. Actual two $250.00 calculations yield $0.00. With two $500.00 streams, expected $5.75 and actual $11.50. Two employees with $250.00 each correctly owe $0.00; a combined household threshold would be an incorrect correction. Reverse the input order and test $499.99/$500.00 and March 31/April 1 as controls.

**False-positive checks.** Per-person compiler eligibility and hybrid allocations are legitimate. The suggested split changes neither legal employee nor Denver eligibility. The local integration test at `test/t1aTaxIntegration.test.ts:658–668` checks a single Denver slice and residence-only negatives, so its positive tax leg does not establish monthly cross-employer aggregation. #277 is reserved for later wage-route review. The baseline grouping issue sits in the tax participant. No runtime case was executed.

### TB-3 · Medium · Credible hypothesis requiring reproduction · Positive contribution deduction can be lost for a spouse with only generated taxable economics

**Requirement and source.** PFA-TAX-010/014, executable semantics §9 and the durable IRA policy at `docs/personal_finance_executable_financial_semantics_v0.1.md:1416–1433` separate funded contribution capacity from eligible tax deduction. The IRS source recorded at `src/rules/contribution2026.ts:12` includes [Publication 590-A](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p590a), including joint-return spousal IRA requirements. Assume a genuinely eligible and committed deduction with explicit compensation and other required facts.

**Path and impact.** `src/simulation/tax/participant.ts:167–173` ingests generated economics independent of canonical `Income`. At `:356–364` federal contribution applicability relies on `sources.get(entry.sourceId)?.ownerId`, where the map contains only `input.incomes` (`:135`). Generated interest or sale facts need not use an Income ID. An eligible IRA deduction can then disappear if that spouse has no separate canonical Income entry in the same rule group, overstating joint tax. The payroll aggregation has a separate fallback at `:371–375` and does not make contribution attribution whole.

**Independent oracle.** Couple files jointly, spouse A has admitted ordinary wages and spouse B has $1,000.00 independently recognized taxable interest from an owned account with a generated operation identity. Supply B's separately funded $500.00 eligible Traditional IRA deduction and all joint compensation facts. In a synthetic 10% bracket without other adjustments, the tax reduction is $50.00. Compare an otherwise identical fixture where the interest is modeled as B's canonical Income, requiring the same recognized amount and deduction in both. Trace the committed contribution row and both group source IDs before attributing any difference to this path.

**False-positive checks.** Opening usage is intentionally excluded (`participant.ts:236`; `test/d1ContributionTax.test.ts:32`). A payable without eligible deduction is also a valid zero. The compiler or funding path could reject the proposed joint facts, or another recognized Income could put B in the group, so the end-to-end symptom remains unconfirmed. #146 owns retirement semantics and #149 the joint tax integration. No runtime case was executed.

### TB-4 · Medium · Spec ambiguity · Target year for a post-year-end non-final payment

**Requirement and source.** PFA-TAX-003/010 and executable semantics §11.4/Golden 3 separate accrual, payment and recognition. [IRS Form 1040-ES](https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-1040-es) is the external reference for an estimated installment paid after the end of the year it covers. Forecast convention and tax-year attribution need an explicit supported contract.

**Path and impact.** `src/simulation/tax/contracts.ts:76–90` gives `taxYear` only to final settlement. `src/application/compiler/tax.ts:170–182` validates final year and generic payment date/amount, while `src/simulation/tax/participant.ts:235,424–452,474–480` assigns non-final credits to the payment date's year. `taxForecastSetup.ts:27–41` emits next-year final instructions and does not author non-final payments. For a client that intends a January estimated installment to apply to last year, the shape cannot express that intent. Assigning it to the current year would distort both years' cash forecasts, even though the current-year credit and cash legs balance.

**Independent oracle.** Start with a $120.00 December payable for 2026 and $200.00 BANK cash. A January 15, 2027 $100.00 payment explicitly designated for 2026 leaves $20.00 prior-year payable, $100.00 cash and $0.00 2027 credit. A later final payment debits cash once for $20.00. Current non-final inputs instead create or apply $100.00 of 2027 credit and leave 2026 payable unchanged until a distinct final instruction. Require either explicit designation or a disclosed unsupported-path diagnostic if this installment is outside the product contract. Also specify whether an explicit same-year final date can settle an interim balance and what happens to later liability for that same year.

**False-positive checks.** `test/payrollWithholding.test.ts:342–366` establishes a same-year estimate and a correctly targeted next-year final refund. `src/application/taxForecastSetup.ts:36–38` already protects the normal authored final route. Existing evidence does not settle the supported scope of prior-year estimates. Resolve through #101/#149 and the tax spec owner before grading this as a runtime defect. No runtime case was executed.

### TB-6 · Medium · Deferred / not reviewed · Integrated benefit and payroll exclusivity

**Requirement and source.** PFA-TAX-015, PFA-DET-016/020 §§5.1, 5.8–5.11 and PFA-INS-013 require distinct gross, premium, withholding, restricted entitlement and funded settlement effects. Which authorized source reaches each branch is part of the active #277/#279 contract. Standalone retirement/Medicare execution under #278 is reserved for route-level review.

**Impact and oracle.** Shared cash or repeated premium attribution could corrupt gross income, wage bases or cash. Expected SSA cash $1,300.00, expense $200.00, zero separate BANK debit for that premium, cleared right and two cleared premium claims. Expected aggregate BANK increase $2,000.00, gross income $2,500.00, premium expense $350.00 and payroll credit $100.00. Use separately sourced regime-specific wages, and keep taxable SSA incomplete until provisional-income facts and law are admitted. A paid FSA reimbursement clears an independent substantiated claim and uses restricted entitlement once.

**False-positive checks.** #266 requalifies original SSA/IRMAA inputs, and #289 supplies native state proof infrastructure. `test/d2/integrated/ssaMedicare/originalSettlement.test.ts:50–105,185` has exact cash and premium checks, including two members. `test/payrollWithholding.test.ts:28` supplies a separate exact payroll example. ## Replay, data size and privacy

- The tax participant transport carries the catalog and fingerprint. `src/rules/tax/catalog.ts:30–43` builds the fingerprint from the complete canonical catalog serialization. `src/simulation/tax/participant.ts:136,166,301–307` threads it into portable inputs and projected qualifications. `src/simulation/tax/contracts.ts:163–180` interns it for diagnostic comparison, and existing summary compaction reduces duplication. A run-scoped catalog bundle is permitted by PFA-TAX-011. Measure emitted bytes for distinct transport and checkpoint records before alleging amplification. Keep exact rule versions, effective dates and occurrence identities in any future reference-based scheme.
- `tax/participant.ts:76,101,209–210,273,330,412` scans or copies receipt and annual economics collections at multiple stages. Cumulative annual recomputation and repeated health original reads can increase work even when the persisted archive grows proportionally. The rule-binding cache and compact diagnostic ledger address specific costs. SSA indexed source reuse (#266) and `indexedHistory.test.ts:188` address provider reads on uninterrupted histories. Cold import appropriately requalifies originals. No timing, allocation or serialized-size measurement was run here.
- Treat employment source labels, filing facts, Medicare return/MAGI evidence, payment dates and proof references as private. Use synthetic IDs, amounts and cutoffs for shared fixtures. Distinguish compact identities from confidentiality: a digest does not make an underlying retained source safe to publish. SSA original-backed receipt tests (`originalSettlement.test.ts:239–255`, `versionedReceipt.test.ts:76,119`) inspect provider-held payload separation and mixed-version replay, alongside exact posting checks elsewhere.
