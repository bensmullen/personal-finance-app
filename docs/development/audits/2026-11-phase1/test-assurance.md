## Authority and oracle types

- `docs/spec-manifest.json` locates the canonical schema (v1.0.4), executable semantics (v0.1.15-draft), and capability specs. The canonical Account and Transaction shapes govern identity and currency. Executable semantics §§3.1–3.4.2, 4.4.1, 5.1–5.4, 11.1–11.11, 14–16.3 and 20 govern half-open periods, funding, recognition, net worth, precision, atomicity, replay and scenario isolation. P08/P13 and P22 govern growth and amortization.
- Requirement IDs below resolve through `docs/specs/verification/requirements-index.json`. DET refers to `deterministic-household-financial-semantics.md`, TAX to `tax-engine.md`, UX to `application-ux.md`, PERF to `performance-and-execution.md`, and ONB to `data-onboarding-and-import.md`, all under `docs/specs/capabilities/`. DET-013 requires independently stated economic effects for each applicable case. DET-023 and TAX-015 add combined D2 evidence and scoped tax character. The approved D2 mechanics are in DET §§5.1–5.11.
- A **literal** oracle supplies precomputed amounts or journal legs from stated facts. A **golden** pins a scenario and can include literal or implementation-derived assertions. A **parity** oracle compares paths, tiers or versions and can preserve a shared error. An **implementation-derived** oracle uses the implementation under review to prepare or calculate expected results. A **property** checks an invariant across input variation. A **journey** checks an ordinary entry point through the browser and may carry any of the preceding oracle types. A conditional proposal may have a strong literal oracle while leaving physical posting, taxation and replay outside its evidence.
- `docs/development/verification-policy.md` §2 and active AL-004 call for independently runnable E2E flows where earlier failures mask unrelated behaviors. AL-002 and AL-003 govern authorization and publication. The only explicit Playwright skips found by the targeted skip search were manual evidence gates in `e2e/ux-evidence.spec.ts:194,240`. I found no evidence that these are false skips. The branch in finding 4 is a separate case of masked evidence.
