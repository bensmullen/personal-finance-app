## Prioritized findings

### 1. Pre-tax-only rollover admits a distribution with previously taxed basis

**High · proven defect by inspection.** The executable contract at lines 1518–1543, DET-004 and INV-022 require preserving tax character and restrict participant-received execution to eligible pre-tax principal. `src/application/compiler/domainMechanics.ts:242–260` requires explicit source basis. In `src/simulation/domainMechanics.ts:223–244`, `executeDomainOperation` checks the rounded basis recovery in the indirect distribution branch. With $1,000.00 owned fair and carrying value, $0.01 known basis and $100.00 gross, exact recovery is $0.001 and rounds to zero. Expected refusal and no cash, credit, receipt or tax change. The inspected branch stages a $100.00 reduction in the source position, $80.00 participant cash, a $20.00 federal tax credit asset and a $100.00 traditional-distribution tax fact. A subsequent full deposit can reverse the distribution tax fact but cannot repair the incorrect admission. Test the same input through authored compilation and the candidate, with the known-zero-basis fixture as control. `test/d1DomainMechanics.test.ts:63–88` uses the pre-tax path. Proposed reproduction was not executed.

### 2. Cent rounding of a unit quote creates loss at zero return

**High · proven defect by inspection.** Executable semantics lines 693–710 and 1027 require separation of principal and returns and explicit rounding boundaries (INV-001/010/017, DET-005/010). `src/application/compiler/investments.ts:228,1221–1265,1397–1408` accepts a decimal unit quote and selects half-up cent rounding. `src/simulation/verticalSlice3.ts:990–1039,1280–1319` uses price as the P23 base and commits the resulting quote. `src/primitives/evaluation.ts:574–582` rounds the closing value, and `src/valuation/index.ts:5–6` multiplies by owned units. With 1,000 units at $0.004 and zero effective annual return, expected closing $4.00 and zero unrealized change. The inspected branch produces a zero quote and a $4.00 loss. Opening value is an exact cent amount, and the compiler does not reject the quote scale. The existing total-loss test and the whole-cent zero-return test use different branches or inputs. Reproduce with a canonical passive holding and no transactions in standalone VS3, household detail and summary. Proposed reproduction was not executed.

### 3. Roth historical splits can reconcile while reversing withdrawal priority

**Medium · credible hypothesis requiring reproduction.** DET-018 §5.3 and TAX-015 distinguish regular basis, vintages, earnings and their clocks. `src/simulation/d2/retirement/rothOrder/index.ts:245–285` checks historical component arithmetic and availability. Lines 312–331 enforce statutory order for new requests. With $100 each of basis and earnings present before a prior ordinary $100 withdrawal, supply history taking $100 from earnings and available pools of $100 basis/$0 earnings. A later nonqualified $100 withdrawal would stage basis and zero income if all other checks pass. Expected refusal of the contradictory history or $100 earnings after lawful prior basis usage. This can shift income or recapture to a different date. `test/d2/retirement/rothOrder/ordering.test.ts:73–98` covers incorrect totals and repeated consumption. Confirm whether the admitted source independently adjudicates historic legal character and hold timing and corrections fixed in the reproduction. Neither the source contract nor the fixture was exercised at runtime.

### 4. A2 historic usage can reconcile while omitting a shared bucket

**Medium · credible hypothesis requiring reproduction.** DET-017 §5.2 and INV-025 require cumulative person/year capacity across eligible plans. `src/simulation/d2/retirement/plans/originalBinding/index.ts:122–155` validates same-person keys and reconciles sums using the historical operations' own key lists. `plans/index.ts:201–208,273–303` separately requires legal scope kinds for new requests. Use a $100 shared cap, a prior eligible $60 403(b) deferral with compensation/additions keys only, an explicitly zero aggregate row and a new $50 same-person TSP ordinary deferral with sufficient headroom elsewhere. Expected $40 shared headroom or a source inconsistency. The inspected reconciliation can derive $0 prior use and permit $50 if the independently published record is admissible. `test/d2/retirement/plans/originalBinding/qualification.test.ts:62–88,128` checks partial history and zero totals against itemization. Confirm with #146 whether source history must attest semantic bucket membership outside the evaluator. The proposed original-book fixture was not executed.

### 5. RMD shortfall has two interpretations before the deadline

**Low · spec ambiguity.** DET §5.4 distinguishes requirement, due date, funded satisfaction and shortfall. `src/simulation/d2/retirement/distributions/index.ts:115–118,234–241,266–270` initializes `deadlineShortfall` at required amount and deducts on-time payments. `overdue` becomes true after the due date. For $700 due December 31 and $500 timely funded, expect $200 still due and overdue false on December 30 and 31. On January 1 the $200 is a missed deadline amount. Payment January 2 reduces remaining to zero and preserves historical $200. The current numeric field already reads $200 before the due date. Clarify its display label before #120 so users do not interpret still-due money as delinquent. `test/d2/retirement/distributions/obligations.test.ts:89–99` asserts the post-deadline case. Neither UI nor the proposed date sequence was executed.

### 6. Fractional-unit residuals need independent conservation tests

**Low · missing/weak test.** Executable semantics lines 1027, 1528–1535 and 1548–1552 separate precision, carrying, fair value and basis (INV-017/022). `src/simulation/verticalSlice3.ts:811–831` accepts a quantity whose product rounds to posting cents. `src/simulation/domainMechanics.ts:155–159,198–201,224–252` divides retirement destination units at finite precision and requires exact multiplication for dividend reinvestment. `src/valuation/index.ts:5–6` uses the raw product. At $3 per unit, 0.333333333333 units rounds to a $1 posting and values at $0.999999999999. Assert residual ownership, aggregate rounding and zero new return or tax across recurring purchases and replay. The reinvestment branch should reject the same approximate $1 product atomically. For a 1-cent dividend at $0.04 per unit, 0.25 units must give one cent of recognized income and acquired basis with zero net wrapper or bank cash change. Existing `test/d1DomainMechanics.test.ts:49–60` and `test/d1IntegratedCloseout.test.ts:125–153` use unit-friendly ratios. A displayed cent error from this residual was not established. Proposed fixtures were not executed.
