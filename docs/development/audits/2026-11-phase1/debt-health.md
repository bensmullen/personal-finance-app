## Prioritized findings

### F1. Independently valid issuer charge can disappear when the source reports a below-minimum payment

**Credible hypothesis requiring reproduction · medium · #149 / #147, S5 #280.** Card expense and closing liability can be understated on a valid issuer interest event when payment funding was already short in the source rather than becoming short in live state.

- **Requirement and branch.** Executable semantics §§4.4.1–4.5 separate recognition, funding and settlement, and DET-019 §5.5 requires the issuer statement and funding facts. `revolving/index.ts:297–318` can return a complete statement and nonzero minimum shortfall. `cardBank/index.ts:196–215` returns `minimum_unpaid` before building the original journal, and lines 300–310, 340–345 schedule neither event for that source. By contrast, lines 370–386 archive a valid original and skip an unfunded debit when the payment qualifies on paper but live BANK is short. With prior card proofs, lines 303–305 can fail preparation instead of emitting just the source diagnostic.
- **Expected and observed statically.** For an independently valid $20 charge on $1,000 principal and a declined $25 issuer minimum, BANK remains $10, card becomes $1,020, expenses rise $20 and settlement count stays zero. In the source-declared shortfall branch, the original is omitted if no separate authority posts it. The adapter's bounded README admits on-time payments and requires independent sources. Confirm with #149 whether the existing trusted publisher is intended to admit original-only documents or needs a new contract. There is no basis for posting an unauthorized partial debit.
- **Reproduction and false-positive check.** Start from `test/d2/integrated/cardBank/scenario.ts` with approved $20 charge, $1,000 opening and the documented minimum. Compare a fully authorized payment with only $10 live cash to a source-declared declined payment with the corresponding zero payment/allocation. Keep valid issuer evidence and place the charge and due date in separate months. Inspect first-month journal, expense, liability and later receipt count on both Person and household routes. `settlement.test.ts:38` and the #290 combined test at line 79 exercise the first branch. Conditional #200 continuity covers partial shortfalls, but supplies no physical journal. #290 adds route qualification and does not remove the early exit. No fixture or replay was run.

### F2. Actual FSA BANK confirmations have a single admissible timestamp

**Spec ambiguity · medium · #148 / #149, coordinate with #279.** A confirmation delivered one millisecond after an actual payment or reimbursement cannot qualify even when available at the run cutoff. This can omit an expense or delay showing reimbursement if the provider contract admits ordinary post-event reporting.

- **Requirement and branch.** Executable §3.2 distinguishes effective time from observation availability, and DET-020 §5.10 / INS-013 require actual, separately sourced payment and reimbursement. In `fsaCash/source.ts:126–130,151–158,214–221`, `stamp` enforces publication ≤ knowledge ≤ economic instant while the actual-payment checks require both timestamps ≥ that instant. Together they imply equality for original paid confirmation and posted deposit. Lines 264–270 include those stamps in the receipt binding, and `src/state/index.ts:2280–2283` also caps receipt knowledge at effective time. `fsaCash/index.ts:123–135,155–178` applies these checks before posting and through history/replay. A later actual confirmation is distinct from a later eligibility or authorization fact.
- **Expected and observed statically.** The equality requirement is provable from the inequalities. Whether a cutoff-qualified historical confirmation may be used at its original economic instant in this adapter is unresolved. Forward execution needs event-known authority. For a separately approved historical route, use the actual effective instant and original knowledge instant, and prohibit earlier runs from seeing the fact. If event-synchronous reporting is the only supported input, surface that coverage limit. Do not backdate a source to make it pass.
- **Reproduction and false-positive check.** Use the $1,500 opening, $1,000 medical payment and $500 approved reimbursement in `test/d2/integrated/fsaCash/scenario.ts:47–62`. Move only the original's publication and knowledge to paid instant +1 ms with a later cutoff. Then restore exact original times and move only the posted deposit confirmation to settlement +1 ms. Equality control should give $1,000 closing cash and $500 net medical expense, with no payroll or entitlement asset. Test source at `settlement.test.ts:273–304` rejects pre-event reporting and late cutoff evidence, while the success fixture uses exact equality. #209's independent source check and #244's v2 compaction do not settle historical timing policy. No case executed. Compare the analogous intentionally exact due-time BANK confirmation in `helocServicing/source.ts:111–115` during Phase 2.

### F3. Coverage gap for missed term-life premium followed by a scheduled benefit

**Missing/weak test · low · #149 and #120 for any surfaced coverage.** A projected death benefit following a failed premium can materially change a stress plan. The expected outcome depends on supported policy semantics and an explicit diagnostic for unsupported lapse behavior.

- **Requirement and branch.** INS-002/003/004/012 and executable §21 require explicit premium funding, coverage period and contingent benefit. `src/application/compiler/domainMechanics.ts:210–219` schedules the benefit from policy dates and the enabled death event. `src/simulation/domainMechanics.ts:112–115,210–219` requires premium funding but the benefit branch independently credits the recipient. The inspected authoring test at `test/d1DomainAuthoring.test.ts:79–88` verifies scheduling and traces with a normally funded fixture. It does not establish the resumed outcome after a premium failure.
- **Expected vs available evidence.** With $49.99 in the owner's BANK against a $50 premium, no $50 debit or overdraft may commit. Place the $100,000 benefit on a later in-coverage date and inspect normal, resumed and cold output for the funding diagnostic and any benefit. A run stopping on failed funding would already prevent the later posting in that run. If a later period can execute, any coverage conclusion needs the admitted policy terms and explicit unsupported-lapse handling. Nothing in the deterministic term-life floor authorizes inferred lapse or a second cash source. Existing beneficiary, federal exclusion and non-federal diagnostic cases address separate risks. No runtime reproduction or assertion of an incorrect payout.

## False-positive checks and data risks

- Variable-rate mortgage in Golden 4 is a general semantic oracle. DET-008 and the fixed private-alpha contract permit explicit gating. Day-31 skip/resume tests address a different schedule boundary from unsupported stub-period accrual.
- #242 separates source-authenticated HELOC service from conditional phase proposals and preserves B1 draw history. #244 adds joint funding and compact FSA replay evidence. Gross service checks and principal-only posting deliberately differ from origination/draw income treatment. An independent complete issuer quote can replace genuinely unavailable rate computation, while conflicting computed interest or bad independent source evidence still gates the branch.
- PFA-DET §5.7 and `529/planTransfers/index.ts:219–230` permit the exact-money residual gate pending sourced allocation. It would be a false positive to demand silent reassignment of a penny. Likewise an FSA entitlement before payroll is permitted uniform coverage, while an actual BANK credit needs the separate posted deposit.
- #266's original SSA version does not elevate legacy receipts to original assurance. #284/#289/#288 and #290 add independent household BANK and economic source checks. Wrong owner/member, late or revoked permissions, mismatched exact signed journals and cold missing publisher have authored negative cases. Legacy Person receipt equality does not imply household permission.
- Compact digests and graph deduplication limit different kinds of duplication. FSA v2 stores a compact full-source pin, with historical v1 full serialized source retained only for exact compatibility. Card configuration carries lookup IDs, while receipt/native attachments retain occurrence-specific evidence. `test/d2/integrated/fsaCash/settlement.test.ts:115–134` and the #290 test at line 239 count serialized growth, not peak heap or provider cost. Preparation still re-reads configured sources and inspects history, e.g. `fsaCash/index.ts:112–134,192–195` and `cardBank/index.ts:293–338`. Repeated qualification may scale with both periods and accumulated occurrences even when wire size is compact. No timing, storage or heap claim is made. Portable artifacts include financial journals and identity references and should be handled as private financial records. Publisher payloads, medical receipts and MAGI originals should remain with their source owners. Use synthetic measurements and retain counts, sizes and failure categories without raw facts.
