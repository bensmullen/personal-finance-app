# Fixture contract and evidence boundaries

`test/fixtures/d2/catalog.json` contains 55 synthetic cases with stable, case-local symbolic identities. `schema.ts` supplies the TypeScript type and strict runtime schema. This private test vocabulary is deliberately not a canonical model or a future compiler interface. Each case identifies its D2 lane, family/variant, normative requirements/anchors, half-open period, source/beneficiary/plan facts, uncertainty and capability boundary.

All monetary quantities are signed integer **USD cents represented as strings**. Quoted amounts are synthetic contract facts, not estimates of tax law or benefit eligibility. Rate conventions are explicit where the existing P22 profile is used; unknown rate/index/legal conventions remain missing inputs. Money calculations in the test-only reconciliation use `bigint`. Nothing uses floating money, randomness, clocks, network requests, real household data or mutable global state.

`decision_required` means a future financial/product contract must be resolved before admitting execution. `not_yet_executable` means the bounded stated behavior already has reusable authority, but this fixture does not prove a supported D2 runtime path. Neither status claims financial-law completeness. Every case has nonempty `unknownOutputs`; absence of an `effects` object means those monetary outputs are unknown, never zero.

Where present, `effects` is a **conditional projection of explicitly supplied economic facts**. Opening accounts, transaction legs, ending balances, cash delta, recognition and net-worth delta are independently stated literals. They are not calculated by a proposed D2 helper. For example:

- A quoted 1,000.00 benefit with an authored 100.00 withholding reconciles 900.00 cash plus a 100.00 tax-credit asset and 1,000.00 gross recognition. Tax expense/liability and eligibility remain unknown; the withholding is not spending.
- A 500.00 same-owner IRA conversion movement changes neither net worth nor consumption. Aggregate IRA taxable amount/basis is deliberately absent. The separate existing D1 same-plan 401(k) conversion case can state 100.00 recovered basis and 400.00 taxable conversion under executable §21; that is not an IRA aggregate/pro-rata rule or a complete mega-backdoor chain.
- P22 zero-rate service and the exact one-remaining-month final payment reconcile principal and interest without implementing a second amortization formula. Failed all-or-nothing required funding commits nothing, including voluntary extra principal.
- A quoted TIPS coupon or EE redemption reconciles only the quoted cash/recognition. No inflation index, EE guarantee or tax election is invented.
- A 529 contribution or eligible same-owner plan transfer preserves consolidated net worth. Qualification, earnings allocation, penalties, recapture and 529-to-Roth statutory values remain unresolved.
- An authored after-tax health/Medicare premium reduces cash and net worth once. Quoted premium arithmetic does not establish Medicare eligibility/IRMAA. FSA reimbursement availability/ownership is unresolved, so no synthetic asset/expense amount is asserted.

Ledger accounts are economic resources or recognition accounts, not wrapper totals. Position, cash and withholding-credit assets are counted once; liabilities subtract from net worth. The test ledger contains no separate wrapper asset that could duplicate its holdings. It excludes unstated tax expense/settlement, fees, valuation changes and other future events as its condition explicitly describes. This gross projection is not an after-tax forecast. Empty transactions represent only explicitly bounded failed/excluded operations; they are not a missing-rule fallback.

`test/d2/verifyCatalog.ts` is a test-only consistency checker, not an authoritative executor. It verifies uniqueness and account-owner references, valid periods, per-transaction balance in the declared currency, exact literal ending balances, cash/recognition/net-worth reconciliation and nonnegative economic balances. `catalog.test.ts` also pins ordinary family/variant coverage, real normative anchors, lane ownership, #103 reuse, capability boundaries and selected independently stated examples. Negative cases detect invalid money/dates, missing uncertainty, duplicate IDs, invalid references, unbalanced legs, changed expected balances/NW, negative balances and postings at the exclusive end.

Reproducibility means the same catalog yields the same reconciliation and leaves inputs unchanged. Reversing account and posting-leg inventories does not change totals. This is an algebraic fixture check, **not** proof that financial operations commute or that incidental order may decide economic precedence. No case executes household compilation, live scheduling, tax settlement or UI paths. Semantic facts in the free-form `terms`/`facts` annotations require independent review; the schema and ledger checks cannot establish their legal truth.

No local tests, typecheck, build, validators, dev server or dependency installation are run in the Codex implementation turn. PR check results are the verification authority and must be reviewed before merge. CI success for this catalog does not close PFA-DET-016–023, Issue #103, U1 #94, D2 or R5. No existing D1/T1A fixtures are modified or imported into this catalog.
