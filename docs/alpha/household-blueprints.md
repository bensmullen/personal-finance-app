# Eight realistic synthetic alpha households — financial blueprints

**Status:** non-normative fixture design, NOT executable test models; as-of **2026-10-01**. **Tracking:** [Issue #108](https://github.com/bensmullen/personal-finance-app/issues/108). **Scope owner:** O1 / R11 alpha exploration. **Source:** invented personas, account figures, flows and identifiers; plausible examples, not empirical representative population data or verified tax law.

## How to use these blueprints
- All amounts in USD and nominal 2026 dollars unless dated. Annual incomes are **gross**, transfers are not expenses, market values are opening snapshots; displayed monthly expense categories are in the adjacent [category matrix](monthly-category-matrix.csv). Amounts are design *targets* subject to deterministic balancing/fixture validation, not reconciled account postings yet.
- Load each model in its own completely isolated demo household; show a one-sentence persona story, assets/debt and cash-flow snapshot, and three clickable questions. Read-only and resettable before enabling separate-copy editing. Users can explore without supplying a bank connection.
- Each person gets explicit age/date of birth, employment status, relationship, tax-residence claims, benefits, liabilities and cash ownership; no shared cash by implication. Household membership does not presume everyone pools income, especially grandparents/working children.
- Every mortgage must have borrower, property, original/current balance, interest basis, next due date, scheduled **principal + interest**, escrow/property tax/insurance separated, extra-payment policy, servicing account, payoff date and refinance scenario. Rental properties require expenses, vacancy, reserve, net proceeds and specific payer.
- All investment accounts list **cash separately** from positions; ETFs and mutual funds dominate. Preserve account type, owner, tax treatment, holdings, dividend policy, position quantities, valuation dates, aggregate basis and (where known) multiple acquisition lots. Example dollar allocations below are opening value targets; actual share quantities and lot prices must be deterministically produced and reconciled.
- Every expense line has a payer/funding account, payee/category and monthly/seasonal frequency; card purchases and subsequent card payments are not double-counted. Payroll benefits and withholding are explicit deductions from gross wages, not spending twice.
- Taxes involving estates, gifts, multi-state real property, rentals/depreciation, equity compensation, split-location work, and pensions should be **capability-gated** unless covered by audited engine rules. Never make up tax-law outcomes. Unsupported areas are still seeded as facts or separately labeled assumptions.
- Base household templates target 24 months of reproducible observed-looking history (2024-10 through 2026-09) plus October opening state and forecasts. This history is generated **synthetically**; historical prices and tax lots should be internally consistent but must not masquerade as authoritative market prices or external records.

## Cross-household controls
- Identity: H01–H08; use fictional stable UUIDs for members, accounts, positions, lots, property, loans and payments; never reuse the same IDs between households.
- **Complexity coverage:** H01 estate/RMDs/pensions/multiple states, H02 pensions/rentals/529s/high-rate debt and linked-but-unpooled people, H03 RSUs/tax-aware investing/401(k), H04 no-investments onboarding, H05 first job/retirement/first rental, H06 NYC filing/student loans/expense stress, H07 mobile lifestyle/multistate uncertainty.
- **Money audit:** exact-decimal schedules, separate employer-paid and household-paid flows, net-vs-gross payroll, actual paid-vs-accrued expenses, transfer identity, payment funded by a named account, isolated intra-family cash, liability and holding identities, gain/basis preservation, book-value vs current market-value provenance.
- **Lot generator target:** for each holding specify account_id/security_id/target_quantity/current valuation/current market value and distribution of dated lots; integer or exact fractional quantities to six places, generated from a fixed seed, ordered deterministic acquisition dates, no lot after snapshot date. Construct prices/bases coherently and reconcile quantity and opening value; mark generated acquisition prices as synthetic. No attempt to recreate historical market ticks from present-day stock prices. Account positions represent **distinct holdings**, even if ticker repeats across accounts.
- **History generator:** base monthly transaction profile in [matrix](monthly-category-matrix.csv), deterministic merchant/memo and 24-month seasonality, payroll and bill calendar, genuine separate card spend/payment, full mortgage amortization, no repeated snapshot-only balance as cash income. Produce opening reconciliation diagnostics for every date/month.
- **Presentation:** no giant raw entry form by default; overview, paychecks/cashflow, assets/investments, debts, taxes, retirement, planning, and explanatory caveat each accessible; guided progressive disclosure. Households are explicitly marked *fictional*; never call a partial retirement/estate/rental result complete.
