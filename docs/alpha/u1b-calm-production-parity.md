## Copy and disclosure contract
- First-level prose: familiar task question, exact supported current money/period if known, one sentence explaining what the selected chart does, one action. Examples: "Your finances today", "How your money changes over time", "Review income", "Money coming in", "Regular spending", "Where your money went this month" **only when those are the accurate recognized/posting bases**.
- Source/caveat disclosure: status such as "Some information is missing" + specific reason/remedy, amounts by person/account/period/recognized-versus-posted basis, relevant tax/withholding/forecast limitations. Do not equate gross payroll with bank deposits or cash inside investments with spendable bank funds.
- Technical disclosure: provenance IDs, solver/ledger terminology, `exclusive` interval convention, pending fingerprints, technical diagnostic codes; must remain accessible for audit, not in default novice prose. Render human month/year in main controls; an actual range with inclusive/exclusive semantics can remain in expanded evidence.
- Never "clean up" an unreadable warning by hiding a hard blocker, changing exact money, merging unrelated concepts or relabeling modeled assumptions as bank transactions.

### Visual-review rubric
A pass means **a first-time user can identify the purpose and primary action at a glance**, a readable meaningful chart and 3–5 current-value indicators do not drown in panels, optional source and advanced detail remain discoverable but collapsed, essential incompleteness remains visible, and default language does not demand familiarity with engine definitions. A fail includes duplicate status walls, more than one competing dominant action, repeated text summaries of the same fact, overlong code-flavored captions, feature parity without layout parity, or inaccessible hidden financial caveats.
