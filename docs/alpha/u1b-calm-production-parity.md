## Copy and disclosure contract
- First-level prose: familiar task question, exact supported current money/period if known, one sentence explaining what the selected chart does, one action. Examples: "Your finances today", "How your money changes over time", "Review income", "Money coming in", "Regular spending", "Where your money went this month" **only when those are the accurate recognized/posting bases**.
- Source/caveat disclosure: status such as "Some information is missing" + specific reason/remedy, amounts by person/account/period/recognized-versus-posted basis, relevant tax/withholding/forecast limitations. Do not equate gross payroll with bank deposits or cash inside investments with spendable bank funds.
- Technical disclosure: provenance IDs, solver/ledger terminology, `exclusive` interval convention, pending fingerprints, technical diagnostic codes; must remain accessible for audit, not in default novice prose. Render human month/year in main controls; an actual range with inclusive/exclusive semantics can remain in expanded evidence.
- Never "clean up" an unreadable warning by hiding a hard blocker, changing exact money, merging unrelated concepts or relabeling modeled assumptions as bank transactions.

## Acceptance checkpoints (independent of green CI)
1. **Baseline and changed-head captures**: Compare exact **production PR HEAD** Overview and Cash at 1440 × 900 and 390 × 844 with same-section Calm prototype at analogous viewport and state. Use genuinely runnable existing synthetic models only; the H04/H08 fictional prototype amounts do not constitute real production fixtures. Caption each comparison with source revision, viewport, sample/status and any unmatched fixture limitations. Screenshot review covers hierarchy, panel density, whitespace, typography, CTA prominence, disclosure defaults and novice readability; functional assertions alone are insufficient.
2. **Task/semantics**: Ask a novice (PM uncoached UAT) to determine "what do I have now?", "how is it changing?", "where did my money go?", "what can I fix?" without reading model internals; identify income/spending/cash/debt differences correctly; preserve exact table and sourcing. Representative desktop + mobile paths include chart month selection, edit/cancel/save, source repair with return, incomplete/payroll tax, stale, no forecast, account with no investments and excessive categorized uses.
3. **Implementation validation**: CI-selected typecheck/unit/build/E2E all pass, plus independently runnable layout/copy/disclosure and financial regressions. Keep strict no-overflow, exact-value and focus assertions. The existing manual UX evidence workflow may be extended to capture Cash/Overview on synthetic model; don't silently insert screenshot churn into routine CI.
4. **Signoff**: Codex reports UI implemented; ChatGPT independently reviews source/CI AND screenshot parity evidence; **PM explicitly accepts** Overview (#170) then Cash (#171) appearance, clarity and discoverability. Close implementation issues only with recorded evidence; final U1/D1 owner acceptance remains #120 and cannot be closed for feature-only green CI.

### Visual-review rubric
A pass means **a first-time user can identify the purpose and primary action at a glance**, a readable meaningful chart and 3–5 current-value indicators do not drown in panels, optional source and advanced detail remain discoverable but collapsed, essential incompleteness remains visible, and default language does not demand familiarity with engine definitions. A fail includes duplicate status walls, more than one competing dominant action, repeated text summaries of the same fact, overlong code-flavored captions, feature parity without layout parity, or inaccessible hidden financial caveats.

**Non-goals:** Pixel-perfect fabricated screenshot tests, rerunning D2 work, unrelated engine or tax semantics, a new data model/writer, new chart construction, new user preference/theme system, inventing H04/H08 forecast outcomes, full U1B-E implementation, and a new UI library.
