## 1. Financial information architecture — semantically correct section labels

**PM clarification (2026-10-17):** the intended *top-level section names* are **Overview · Cash · Investing · Real Estate · Other Assets · Debt · Planning** — **NOT** an additional `Charts & insights` page or a five-area `Cash Flow / Net Worth` wrapper. Users open the financial section to see its **primary chart first**, with record/source details below. This supersedes the previous five-area navigation proposal without changing the canonical 62-family one-writer registry. At 390px show Overview, Cash, Investing, Planning and **More**; More exposes Real Estate, Other Assets, Debt, Household & People and Save/Return.

| Visible destination | Chart-first role and nested financial concepts | Single authoritative writer (no additional source) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Overview** | Full simulation-horizon net worth / total assets / total liabilities **main graph**, followed by sourced current position and concise action choices | Read-only overview; edits open actual owner |
| **Cash** | Cash balance line + monthly signed change columns; income-vs-expenses bars; period-specific horizontal **gross-income allocation**. Beneath: bank balances, Income, Spending, insurance costs, transfers and pay deposit evidence. **Cash account balances are not cash flow**, though cash movement belongs here by task intent | Existing Money → Accounts/Income/Spending/Insurance & coverage editors, re-used contextually |
| **Investing** | Actual investment-account balance/time-series when supported, with attribution chart only when provenance can reconcile. Honest no-investments empty chart state for H04. Taxable brokerage, bonds/CDs according to container, 401(k), IRA, HSA asset positions. No phantom employer enrollment | Net Worth → Investments & retirement → owning account/holding editor |
| **Real Estate** | Mortgage **monthly interest + scheduled/extra principal stacked columns** prominent; property value and liability separate, home equity derived and not double-counted. Property and mortgage are shown together but remain two sourced financial objects | Net Worth → Property & other assets for property, Net Worth → Debt for contract/funding/payment |
| **Other Assets** | Only actual vehicles, physical property or business/other assets with sourced values; if none have value history, show chart-size truthful empty state instead of made-up attribution | Net Worth → Property & other assets, filtered by class |
| **Debt** | Liability-balance trajectory primary chart; loans, mortgage, auto, card, student and HELOC terms and payment funding are in source details; mortgage chart is accessible in Real Estate | Net Worth → Debt (one authoritative liability writer) |
| **Planning** | Retirement cash-source stacked columns and forecast lifecycle/status first; planning ages, horizon, events, assumptions and Compare Plans accessible below. Pensions/Social Security are income streams, not investment balances | Plan → Current Plan/Life Events/Assumptions and #105-independent alternatives only when implemented |

**Terminology:** Net worth = assets less liabilities, not a synonym for "assets". Home equity = property value less associated secured debt, not another positive asset. Mortgage principal paid = financing cash outflow and debt reduction, not consumption. Cash **stock** differs from recognized income and period cash **flow**. Retirement plan balances belong under Investing; pensions/SS income shown under Planning only when supported. Multiple read-only chart appearances shall never create duplicate writable financial inputs. **The user-facing nav is not a migration of canonical editor authority.**

**Visual design checks implemented in the HTML reference:** chart panel appears before the source/details panel in each section with supported data; the retired `Charts & insights` page and navigation tab are removed. Histogram columns now occupy most of each period band (income/spending paired columns 17px per series on a 40px month pitch; mortgage 25px on a 29.4px month pitch; retirement 41px on a 49px year pitch), retaining small separation for readability. SVG period selection and selected-income-bar → embedded gross-income breakdown stay on the **Cash** page. At 390px the graph frame scrolls internally if needed; the page itself does not overflow. These pixel values refer to the static design SVG viewBox, not final responsive production pixel units.

### V4 example (design semantics)

For a selected month, a user may see `Taxes | Mortgage | Retirement payroll | Food | ... | Disposable income`, with ordered components based on **value** and disposable remainder ALWAYS on the right, even when smaller than another category. Taxes/premiums not known? Show **Unreconciled** as a named, explicitly partial segment, not as disposable. Additional bank/credit funding for spending above gross income appears as a **distinct source** and excess-outflow note, not a fictitious part of gross wages. The user can switch the denominator/basis only with an explicit label (gross wages vs household receipts) and the mapping must reconcile.

### V5 equity integrity

Mortgage bars show **interest paid**, **scheduled principal paid** and **extra principal paid**. Cumulative principal paid is a real lender-debt reduction; *total home equity* also depends on home value, additional liens, appreciation, fees and the date. If complete property-value history isn't modeled, show **Cumulative principal repaid** rather than **Equity gained**. This is essential to be trustworthy to a knowledgeable homeowner.

## 3. Bounded data adapter contract (financial semantics outside UI)

**Known current readouts (checked against main):**

- `src/application/householdProjection.ts` `HouseholdForecastPoint` already includes `cash`, `assets`, `totalAssets`, `liabilities`, `totalLiabilities`, `netWorth`, `statementIncome`, `statementExpenses`, `operatingCashFlow`, `investingCashFlow`, `financingCashFlow`, `debtPrincipalReduction`, `investmentContributionPrincipal`, `fees`, `unrealizedGain`, period boundaries, and trace refs. `PersonalHouseholdForecastReadModel` includes opening snapshot, requested/reached boundaries and `status: completed|incomplete|unavailable`. **actualHistoryAvailable=false**: do not label generated projection as observed historical performance.
- `src/application/monthlyMoney.ts` exposes replay-verified **`inspectMonthlyMoney(point, model)`** with `categories`, `additionalFunding`, `unallocated`, `basis`, `receipts`, `mortgages`, and employee contribution detail. Its `withholding` is currently **`not_modeled`** and result **status is `incomplete`**. Do NOT treat the returned `unallocated` as guaranteed disposable cash. An income allocation chart needs a separate reconciled, capability-aware read-only adapter and may show partial results while #101 is outstanding.
- `src/application/personalMvp.ts` `LiabilityOccurrenceReadModel` already includes dates, contractual/current payment, current interest, scheduled vs extra principal, ending principal, funding status and trace refs. This is a candidate for V5, but actual **paid/funded** amount must be checked, not just scheduled.
- **No verified complete V6/V7 by-source or by-attribution read model** found in this targeted review. Treat as **source-readiness research**, not permission to implement invented decomposition.

**Preferred architecture:** one **pure, read-only visualization selector** layer returning typed, currency/period/basis-labeled series from validated authoritative forecast/read models. It may group month buckets to annual periods, derive simple identities validated against outputs, and request *one selected period's* replay evidence lazily. It may not run a second financial ledger, reclassify tax/benefit events, or create actuarial/market assumptions. For chart-specific slices requiring source changes, pre-authorize the **exact** `src/application/...` adapter path plus its bounded tests in that phase; do not hide domain changes under `ui/**`.

**Proposed output shape (illustrative, not schema-authoritative):**

```ts
type Period = { start: string; end: string; granularity: "month"|"year" };
type ChartValue = { exact: string; currency: string };
type ChartSample = { period: Period; series: Record<string, ChartValue | null>;
  status: "complete"|"partial"|"unsupported"|"stale"; sourceRefs: string[] };
type ChartResult = { metric: string; planId?: string; basisFingerprint: string;
  requestedStart: string; requestedEnd: string; reachedThrough?: string;
  samples: ChartSample[]; unsupportedPeriods: Period[]; diagnostics: string[] };
```

**Contract QA:** net worth reconciliation on each point; month-to-annual aggregation uses ending snapshot for *stocks* (balances/net worth), sum of monthly values for *flows* (income/expenses/interest/principal), never sum month-end balances; financing sources separated from true income; no negative stacked positive segment; changing extra payment only affects supported recomputed scenario results; no source change after Cancel; no fake period after `reachedThrough`.

## 4. Unified chart interaction and accessibility contract

- Desktop 1440: a clear, moderate-height main chart in relevant section, with a short metric legend and selected-period explanation below. Dashboard contains *previews* for V1/V2/V3, not 7 full charts before the user's first task.
- Narrow/mobile 390: one chart visible at a time, swipe-free primary axis; a named month/year selection control for touch/keyboard, tap targets, concise legend and accessible **data-table or text series view**. Scrolling the page must not accidentally pan/zoom the chart.
- Long horizons: default annual for V1/V2/V7 (and V6 if >5-year); enable monthly after explicit zoom/range selection. V3/V5 default monthly in a manageable rolling window; annual aggregation available. Avoid thousands of DOM nodes: downsample/virtualize only for the overview *presentation*, preserving exact selected-point drilldown.
- Tooltip/focus always shows person/account scope, date, amount, **gross/net and stock/flow basis**, compared plan and source if materially relevant. Accessible textual interpretation and exact value for selected month/year. Keep consistent monetary color language; V2 uses signed bars and redundant positive/negative text, not red/green alone.
- A selected income period may **optionally** open V4's breakdown as a focusable inline detail/sheet, preserving the selected period and the user's return location. This **is a documented UX experiment**, not mandatory acceptance for #120; basic V4 accessible selection and explicit open action are mandatory once V4 is supported.
- Every chart handles **loading, empty/no investments, partial, unsupported, stale, failed, canceled and out-of-range** as applicable. No invented line segment over unsupported years, and no interactive chart implying an unsupported financial decision is computed.
