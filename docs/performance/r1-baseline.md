# R1 performance baseline

The committed `benchmarks/r1-engineering-reference.json` artifact is historical/reference-only evidence from an older head on Node 24. Its overlapping compilation phases, model-export surrogate, and heap-delta-only memory evidence are superseded. The retained numbers have not been rewritten. It is neither current-head Node 22 evidence nor an approved budget or R3 comparison baseline.

Capture uses two warmups and five measured runs per synthetic fixture by default. `PERF_WARMUPS` (0–10), `PERF_RUNS` (1–20), and `PERF_OUTPUT` may override those values. The v2 artifact retains the actual captured Git head/runtime, raw records, context, phase summaries, requested/reached horizon and actual result status. Heavy capture measures full-horizon computational execution: `completed` and `incomplete` results are eligible only when `reachedThrough` equals `requestedHorizon.end`. Missing boundaries, truncated execution, unavailable/unsupported/error results and thrown executions reject capture, including warmups and scaling probes. Post-T1A capability diagnostics may explicitly gate tax-affected outputs while execution reaches the full horizon; such samples retain status=`incomplete` and are valid for performance/resource evidence only. They do not establish financial completeness. Ordinary CI remains the authority for financial correctness and invariants. CPU is aggregate process user/system execution time, not a financial value or budget. Absolute sampled heap after execution/diagnostic collection is retained alongside signed heap delta; the sample is not a peak. Local Node and browser-main execution are `not_metered`; no zero-dollar provider cost is fabricated.

Primary Golden/realistic/stress records remain separate from four bounded scaling probes (12/24 months × two supported recurring-operation/entity scales). Each probe retains horizon, actual recurring-operation count and model entity count. Golden is unchanged. Realistic adds distinct P08 assumption bindings, multiple income-termination events, accounts/positions, monthly investment purchases/transfers, and mortgage extra-principal dependencies. Stress scales those supported mechanics over 180 months. The coverage inventory identifies actual evidence owners: root forecasts are measured by capture; bounded realistic lower-return comparison execution is covered by CI and controlled heavy validation, while stress comparison execution is heavy-only. Comparison intents in scaling probes have structural coverage only; no probe comparison execution is claimed. Earlier-retirement comparison and contention-policy coverage are not claimed. Timing never establishes a correctness threshold or an approved p95 budget.

Browser-main diagnostics are interactive/in-memory and available in `Settings > Advanced`; they are not a retained representative-device baseline. Attribution expires after the matching forecast commit; unrelated navigation, charts and explanation work are not attributed to that forecast. React/chart timings are profiler-dependent and display as N/A when unavailable. Forecast transport serialization is explicitly `not_applicable`: there is no worker/server transport. Model export is not a surrogate. Server/cloud execution remains `not_implemented/not_measured`; placement recommendations are deferred until approved budgets exist.

T1A tax participant execution, capability diagnostics and output completeness gating participate in the normal deterministic Golden, realistic, stress and scaling forecast paths. Missing authored tax facts or verified law remain gaps in full target-cohort tax completeness; the inventory does not claim full tax-law coverage. Financially incomplete results can still be computationally full-horizon. Capture preserves that distinction without changing tax calculations, PFA-TAX-008/009 diagnostics, output capabilities or application/UI lifecycle semantics.

GitHub ordinary CI executes the realistic application fixture and its lower-return comparison on one-month requests, plus a targeted two-month realistic compiled-engine check for transfers and extra principal. Stress is deliberately computationally complex even on one month: CI only compiles a bounded stress request and inspects its model, recurring operations, investment instructions, debt/event/scenario declarations and unsupported gaps. It does not execute a stress forecast, compiled-engine run or scenario comparison; compilation is structural evidence, not runtime evidence. Scaling tests check matrix declarations, counts, coverage and one-month compilation without forecasting probes. Injected application/engine-owned clocks and sinks prove diagnostic failure isolation; normal Advanced/N/A/context and stale-attribution E2E coverage remains. No larger timeouts, retries, skips or correctness timing thresholds hide expensive execution.

Codex does not run capture, tests, typecheck, builds, Playwright or validators. After ordinary CI is green, the manual `engineering-validation` workflow with profile `performance` on Node 22 owns full primary/scaling executions, including the unchanged 180-month stress primary request, and uploaded timing/resource artifacts/history. `benchmarks/capture.ts` enforces computational full-horizon execution through `requireFullHorizonPerformanceForecast`; `requireCompletedPerformanceForecast` continues to require strict financial completion. After all timed warmup/measured loops, capture also executes one one-month lower-return comparison validation for each realistic/stress representative. Capture fails before writing a successful artifact unless the comparison and every declared alternative have status `completed` or `incomplete`, with the alternative comparison boundary equal to the intended bounded end. Intent identity, declared change, alternative count/name and boundary checks remain enforced. The separate comparison validation retains actual statuses and only non-financial applicability, horizon, scenario identity/change kinds and alternative comparison boundary; it retains no financial results or deltas and is neither a timing/resource sample nor a full-horizon comparison claim. The first external repaired-head smoke should use warmups=0 and runs=1. Current-head post-T1A Node 22 evidence remains pending until the manual performance engineering-validation rerun succeeds; ordinary CI alone does not restore it. No latency, memory, throughput, cost or convergence budget is established by this repair.
